Three things we do better

1 · A protocol engine that cannot lie about its rules

  • 58 built-in protocols across industrial, telecom, IoT and Internet families
  • Protocol families: pick szl4 once and every message type of that protocol decodes — dispatched per member, each with its own framing rule
  • Framing: fixed length, length-prefixed (with explicit "does the length include itself / header / trailer" semantics) and terminator-delimited, plus a max-frame guard
  • A fixture-verified gate: every rule must prove it decodes exactly one whole frame, with no residue. Rules that can't prove themselves don't ship.
  • Never silently drops data: resynchronises after garbage, waits on half-frames, hands unrecognised frames to you verbatim
  • Rules are files, not hard-coded logic — with your own rules folder

2 · A transfer engine built for the awkward cases

  • Large files, transferred directly
  • Huge numbers of small files, aggregated
  • Interrupted transfers, resumed
  • Copies of detected media links go to whatever tool you prefer — we don't force ours

3 · One tool for host and WSL

  • The same app on Windows and inside WSL2 Ubuntu
  • Host-side and WSL-side debugging share one toolbox
  • We document exactly where the WSL experience differs — no surprises

Privacy

  • No telemetry by default.
  • No third-party advertising SDKs — the in-app promotion slots are our own content.
  • Diagnostics are off by default, and when off they neither upload nor write anything.
  • Remote requests (ad manifest, update check) happen only if you configure them.

Responsible use

Yutide is a general-purpose networking tool. Download only what you are entitled to download and comply with the terms of the sites you use and the laws that apply to you.

  • We do not circumvent DRM or technical protection measures, and we do not bypass paid access.
  • Built-in media rules are protocol-level only (HLS/DASH/direct media). Site-specific adapters are user-supplied.
  • Copying a detected resource link merely exposes a URL your browser already fetched — using it is your decision.
